# Video editor

A project from Record Project opens in the video editor. The window has a settings rail on the left (Cursor, Clicks, Keystrokes, Audio, Camera, Background, Motion Blur), the preview in the middle with playback under it, and the timeline at the bottom: a ruler, a zoom lane, and a clip lane with pictures of the recording. The preview draws each frame from the project through `frameState`, so the pointer and the click rings are layers over the untouched `screen.mp4`. Space plays and pauses, and Settings > Shortcuts lists every key the editor answers from the same table. The clip lane holds the clips the finished video keeps: Command-B splits at the playhead, Delete removes a clip, dragging a clip's edge trims it, and each cut has a mark above and below the lane. The zoom lane holds zooms on the finished video's timeline: a click adds a 2 s zoom at 200%, a drag adds one over its stretch, edges resize, the body moves, and a right click opens Level, Apply Level to All, Mode, Split at Playhead, Duplicate, Remove Zoom and Remove All Zooms. A picked zoom takes the settings panel over. The camera moves in and out over the project's move duration (default 0.78 s) on smootherstep, and zooms less than two moves apart hand over without returning to 100%. A Follow Cursor zoom holds while the pointer stays in the hold area (default the middle half of the view) and drifts after it once it leaves, through a critically damped spring that settles in the catch-up time (default about 0.68 s); a Manual zoom holds the spot picked in its panel, where a picture of the recording at the zoom's middle shows the view as a frame to drag, click or move with the arrow keys. Animation Settings, a button in the zoom panel and an item in the zoom's menu, opens a panel over the settings column with Duration, Hold area and Catch-up; they apply to every zoom and are stored in `edits.zoomAnimation` (an older file reads the defaults). Full Screen Preview hides the settings and the timeline. A project is a package, `<name>.cornermarkvideo`, holding `project.json`, the source video and any added picture; a plain folder from before packages opens the same way. Command-S saves, Shift-Command-S (File > Save As) copies the package and edits the copy, and closing the window writes changed edits to `project.json` without asking. A video file (MP4 or MOV) opens as a new project: it is copied into a package in the save folder, never encoded again, and listed in History. Export, top right, renders the edited video through the same `frameState` with VideoToolbox's hardware H.264 encoder and lists it in History with a Quick Access card. Export Current Frame as Image (Shift-Command-E) draws the frame at the playhead the same way and hands it on as a screenshot.

## Sub-features

- `editor-open` opens a project folder from the Quick Access card's Edit, History's Edit, or `edit-recording?path=<folder>`. A plain `.mp4` keeps Play.
- `editor-layout` is the rail, the panel, the preview, the playback row, and the timeline lanes.
- `editor-sections` switches the panel. Cursor has Show cursor, Size, Style (White, Black, Dot), Motion (Natural, Smooth), Hide when not moving and Hide when typing; Clicks has Press effect, Ripple, and the ring's Color, Size, Style (Outline, Filled) and Motion (Grow, Still); Background, Camera, Keystrokes and Audio have their own settings; a section whose recording has no camera, keys or sound says so and names the setting to turn on before recording, and for an opened video file it says the file has none. Motion Blur picks what blurs (Off, Zooms, Zooms + cursor) and Intensity, the shutter angle from 30° to 360° (default Zooms + cursor at 180°).
- `editor-cursor` is how those settings draw the pointer. Smooth runs the recorded path through a critically damped spring. Hide when not moving fades the pointer 1.5 s into a rest. Hide when typing fades it at a key press without Command or Control until it next moves. Command-Z and Shift-Command-Z undo and redo each change, and one slider drag is one step.
- `editor-clicks` is how the Clicks settings draw a click. Press effect shrinks the pointer to 0.8 of its size in 0.05 s and lets it grow back in 0.18 s; it is off while Show cursor is off. Ripple is the ring the recording's settings started with (`edits.clicks`), alive 0.4 s, centred where the pointer is drawn at the click (the spring's position under Smooth), and drawn whether or not the pointer shows. A recording without clicks says so and turns the settings off. Every change is one undo step.
- `editor-camera` changes the recorded camera: Show camera, Shape, Size, a 3×3 Position grid and Mirror camera. `project.json` keeps them in `edits.camera` (`position` is one of the nine cells; an older file's `corner` still reads). The full-screen moments from recording stay. Preview and export draw the bubble through the same `CameraLayout` and painter.
- `editor-keystrokes` changes the key badge: Show keystrokes, Keys (Shortcuts or All keys), Size, Style and a 3×3 Position grid, stored in `edits.keystrokes`. All keys can only show keys the recording kept.
- `editor-audio` turns the microphone and system audio on or off and sets each level, stored in `edits.audio` as linear gains. Export mixes the tracks at those gains, leaves a track that is off out, and writes no sound track when both are off. The live preview lays `mic.m4a` and `system.m4a` beside the picture at the same gains, but only an editor on screen plays sound: `playsSound` is off in a harness and in a scripted instance, which never orders the editor in, and the player stays muted.
- `editor-background` puts the recording on a background: None, gradients with Show more and Show less, wallpapers with Add image, and plain colours, then Padding, Shadow and Corners under them. Padding grows the video on every side, so the export panel's sizes grow with it. An added image is copied into the project folder. Every change is one undo step, a slider drag included.
- `editor-aspect` is the Ratio menu at the top of the frame settings: Auto, 1:1, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 4:5, 3:4, 9:16, and Custom with a width and a height. A ratio grows the video around the padded recording and centres it; without a background the rest is black bars. The export panel's sizes follow, named by the short side, and the long side comes from the ratio, so 1080 at 9:16 is 1080 x 1920. Each pick and each typed number is one undo step. `project.json` stores it as `edits.background.aspect`; an older file is Auto.
- `editor-playback` is Space, Go to Start, Go to End, clicking or dragging the timeline, arrow keys on the focused timeline, and the volume, which is off for a recording without sound.
- `editor-clips` is splitting, deleting and trimming. Command-B or the scissors button splits the clip under the playhead. A click picks a clip; Delete or the trash button removes it, or the clip under the playhead when none is picked, and a video keeps at least one. A drag on a clip's edge trims it on the source's frame grid, and the playhead rides the edge. On the focused timeline Option-Left and Option-Right move the clip's start, Option-Command its end, Shift a second at a time. Command-Z and Shift-Command-Z undo and redo every edit, a drag or a run of key presses as one step. `project.json` stores the clips as `edits.clips`; an older file without them keeps the whole recording. Export writes only the clips.
- `editor-fullscreen` is Full Screen Preview. Esc or the same button returns.
- `editor-save` writes changed edits to `project.json` on Command-S, before an export and on close, and never touches the source video. The header says Edited, then Saved. A close the viewer asks for stays open when the file cannot be written. Save As copies the package; a scripted instance names the copy `<name> copy` beside it instead of showing a panel.
- `editor-open-video` opens an MP4 or MOV file: `open?path=<file>`, Finder's Open With, or File > Open Video. The new package lands in the save folder with the file under its own name, and History lists it. Cursor and Keystrokes say the video has no tracks of its own; Audio says whether its own sound plays and exports.
- `editor-shortcuts` is every key the editor answers, in one table, `EditorShortcuts`: the window's keys, the focused timeline's, the focused zoom lane's and a manual zoom's focused target frame's, and the File and Edit menu items' key equivalents all read it. Settings > Shortcuts lists it read-only under the editable global shortcuts, in four groups (playback, clips, zooms, project and export), each row the command's name, where it works, and its keys as caps. Command-E opens the export panel, Shift-Command-E exports the current frame, Control-Command-F toggles Full Screen Preview. There is no timeline zoom, so none is listed.
- `editor-export-frame` is File > Export Current Frame as Image, or Shift-Command-E: the frame at the playhead drawn by `ExportFrameRenderer`, sharp (one sample, no motion blur), from the source frame the export's own reader picks, at `ExportPlan`'s original size. It is handed to the app as a capture: History lists a PNG, and After capture decides the rest, a Quick Access card by default. The log has `video frame exported project= t= size= kind=`. Nothing is uploaded.
- `editor-export` is Export: the panel (Resolution, Quality, the size estimate), the progress with Cancel, and the export's card with Copy, Save, Play and Close. Export saves changed edits first. A cancelled or failed export leaves no file. The export lives in its History entry's folder until Save copies it to the save folder.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Record Project, then choose **Edit** on the recording's Quick Access card.
- Select the recording in Capture History and choose **Edit**, or double-click it.
- Open `cornermark-dev://edit-recording?path=<project folder>`.

## Driving it with link.sh

Preconditions:

- The baseline in [the map](./README.md) holds.
- The instance has a project folder. Record one from the synthetic display as [Recording](./recording.md) describes.

- **Open by link.** Run `$V/link.sh edit-recording "<root>/saved/Cornermark Recording <date>"`. The output has `video editor opened project=<name> seconds=<s> size=<w>x<h> pointerSamples=<n>`, then `present window=<name> visible=false appActive=false`, and `"onScreenWindowsMax":0`. A folder outside the root is copied to `<root>/import/` first.
- **Export by link.** Run `$V/link.sh edit-recording "<project folder>" export`. It sends `edit-recording?path=<folder>&export=1`, which opens the editor and exports with the default choices. The log has `video export started ... estimatedBytes=` and then `video exported ... frames= seconds= projectSeconds= bytes= hardware=true encoder=com.apple.videotoolbox.videoencoder.ave.avc renderSeconds=`. The file is in `<root>/History/<entry>/`, and `<root>/History/.incoming` is empty afterwards.
- **Open a video file.** Run `$V/link.sh open <file.mov>`. `link.sh` copies the file into `<root>/import/`, and the log has `video opened from file file=<name> project=<name>.cornermarkvideo seconds= size= fps=`, then `video editor opened`. The package is in `<root>/saved/`.
- **Refusal.** A folder without `project.json` is refused by `link.sh` before anything is sent.
- **Everything else.** The window is built but never drawn in the instance, so the rest is harness-only. Run `swift run HarnessShell` and read the sections `video editor: opening`, `video editor: layout and sections`, `video editor: playback`, `video editor: full screen preview`, `video editor: edits and the project file`, `video editor: cursor size, style, motion and hiding`, `video editor: split, delete and trim`, `video editor: preview frame time`, and `video editor: playing across a cut`. The `CUT` line says how far the live player was from the playhead after a cut. Snapshots of the clips are `video-editor-clips-split.png`, `video-editor-clips-selected.png` and `video-editor-clips-selected-dark.png`. Snapshots are `build/harness/shell/video-editor.png`, `video-editor-dark.png`, and `video-editor-min.png`. The Cursor panel is in `video-editor-cursor.png`, `video-editor-cursor-dark.png` and `video-editor-cursor-off.png`. The section `video editor: click press and ripple` covers Clicks; its snapshots are `video-editor-clicks.png`, `video-editor-clicks-dark.png`, `video-editor-clicks-min.png`, `video-editor-clicks-cursor-off.png` and `video-editor-clicks-none.png`.

## Gotchas

- The instance never orders the editor in, so `visible=false` is correct. A `visible=true` line means isolation is broken.
- The preview's picture and pointer are Core Animation layers. `cacheDisplay` leaves them out, so a snapshot of the window renders the preview's layer tree on top. `editorImage` in `ChecksVideoEditor.swift` does that.
- An offscreen window cannot enter macOS full screen. The harness checks the layout and the dark appearance of Full Screen Preview, and the film resizes the window to the display's size in its place.
- The `PERF` line measures the live decoder and one tick, layer commit included, on a 60 Hz timer in the harness. Core Animation's compositing runs outside the main thread and is not in the number.
